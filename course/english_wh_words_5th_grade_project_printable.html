What are WH-words?

WH-words are question words. We use them to ask for information.

people
things
places
time
reasons
the way things happen
Super short rule
What = a thing
Who = a person
Where = a place
When = time
Why = a reason
How = a way
Meet the WH-words team!

What ❓📦

Thing or action

Use it when: you want information about something.

Example: What is your favorite color?

Picture clue: a mystery box

Who ❓👦

Person

Use it when: you want to know the person.

Example: Who is your best friend?

Picture clue: a smiling student

Where ❓🏠

Place

Use it when: you want to know the location.

Example: Where do you live?

Picture clue: a little house

When ❓⏰

Time

Use it when: you want to know the day or the time.

Example: When is your birthday?

Picture clue: a clock

Why ❓😂

Reason

Use it when: you want to know the cause.

Example: Why are you laughing?

Picture clue: a happy face

How ❓🚲

Way or feeling

Use it when: you want to know the method, condition, or feeling.

Example: How do you go to school?

Picture clue: a bicycle ride

Funny classroom examples 😄

  1. What is the monkey eating?
    Answer: It is eating a banana!
  2. Who is singing in the classroom?
    Answer: Sara is singing!
  3. Where is the school bag?
    Answer: It is under the desk!
  4. When do penguins sleep?
    Answer: They sleep at night!
  5. Why is the baby chick jumping?
    Answer: Because it is excited!
  6. How do you make a sandwich?
    Answer: First, put cheese and tomatoes in the bread. Then eat it!
