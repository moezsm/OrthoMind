## 1) WHAT are WH-words?

WH-words are question words.  
We use them to ask about:

- **people**
- **things**
- **places**
- **time**
- **reasons**
- **the way something happens**

---

## 2) Meet the WH-words Team! 🌟

| WH-word | Meaning | When do we use it? | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What** | asks about a thing or an action | when we want information about something | **What** is your favorite color? |
| **Who** | asks about a person | when we want to know the person | **Who** is your best friend? |
| **Where** | asks about a place | when we want to know the location | **Where** do you live? |
| **When** | asks about time | when we want to know the day or time | **When** is your birthday? |
| **Why** | asks for a reason | when we want to know the cause | **Why** are you laughing? |
| **How** | asks about the way something happens | when we want to know the method, condition, or feeling | **How** do you go to school? |

---

## 3) Easy reminders with funny picture clues 🎨

### **What** ❓📦
Think: **a thing**

```text
   ______
  / ____ \
 | |WHAT| |
 | |____| |
  \______/
```

**Example:** What is in the box?

---

### **Who** ❓👦
Think: **a person**

```text
   \O/
    |
   / \
```

**Example:** Who is wearing the red hat?

---

### **Where** ❓🏠
Think: **a place**

```text
    /\
   /  \
  /____\
  | [] |
  |____|
```

**Example:** Where is the cat?

---

### **When** ❓⏰
Think: **time**

```text
   .----.
  | 12  |
  | 6   |
   '----'
```

**Example:** When do you go to school?

---

### **Why** ❓😂
Think: **a reason**

```text
  \(^_^)/
    | |
```

**Example:** Why are you happy?

---

### **How** ❓🚲
Think: **a way**

```text
   __o
 _ \<_
(_)/(_)
```

**Example:** How do you go to school?

---

## 4) Super short rule ✅

- Use **What** for a **thing**
- Use **Who** for a **person**
- Use **Where** for a **place**
- Use **When** for **time**
- Use **Why** for a **reason**
- Use **How** for **the way something happens**

---

## 5) Funny classroom examples 😄

1. **What** is the monkey eating?  
   *It is eating a banana!*

2. **Who** is singing in the classroom?  
   *Sara is singing!*

3. **Where** is the school bag?  
   *It is under the desk!*

4. **When** do penguins sleep?  
   *They sleep at night!*

5. **Why** is the baby chick jumping?  
   *Because it is excited!*

6. **How** do you make a sandwich?  
   *First, put cheese and tomatoes in the bread. Then eat it!*
