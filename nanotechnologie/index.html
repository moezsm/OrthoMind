What is it?

  • Manipulating matter between 1 and 100 nm
  • Idea launched by Richard Feynman in 1959
  • New physical and chemical properties
  • Crossroads of physics, chemistry and biology

Nanomaterials

  • Graphene: a single layer of carbon atoms
  • Carbon nanotubes: ultra-strong
  • Silver and gold nanoparticles
  • Quantum dots for displays

Applications: Medicine

  • Targeted drugs against cancer
  • Early diagnosis with nanosensors
  • RNA vaccines in lipid nanoparticles
  • Antibacterial dressings

Electronics, Energy, Environment

  • Processors etched at a few nanometers
  • Faster, longer-lasting batteries
  • More efficient solar panels
  • Nano-filters to purify water

✔ Advantages

  • Precise, less invasive medicine
  • Light and very strong materials
  • Energy savings
  • Device miniaturization
  • Water and air decontamination

✘ Disadvantages

  • Possible health toxicity
  • Poorly understood environmental impact
  • High production costs
  • Privacy risks
  • Insufficient regulation
